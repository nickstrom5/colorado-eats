# co-eats

Three things built from one pipeline (modeled on `../wi-eats/`, which is never modified):

1. **The web leaderboard**: every Colorado restaurant ranked on several boards (Top and Bottom), published as a private claude.ai Artifact
   from `site/` (generated by `scripts/make-leaderboard.py`). It uses the UCSD Google Local 2021 snapshot for ratings, labeled "2021"
   everywhere, so `site/` never goes in a public repo.
2. **Colorado Eats**, a free iPhone and iPad app (`ColoradoEats/`, XcodeGen `project.yml`). No Google-derived data: places come from Overture
   Maps open data and official records, ratings and hours live from Apple Maps' own place card.
3. **The website** in `docs/` (generated by `scripts/make-site.py`): landing page, six guides, 16 city pages, the web app at `/explore/`,
   privacy, terms. QA with `scripts/qa-site.py`.

See `CLAUDE.md` for the rules and commands, and `playbook/` for sources and calibration (`12-sources.md`), the App Store listing
(`06-app-store-listing.md`), the App Review reply and recording script (`13-app-review-reply.md`) and the eatsranked.com hub entry (`hub/`).

## Official records
- **Boulder County Public Health inspections** (data.colorado.gov, public domain): the only bulk inspection results in Colorado. Shown as
  recorded (Pass / Re-Inspection Required / Closure, with risk points), never as a grade of ours.
- **Colorado liquor licenses** (Department of Revenue, data.colorado.gov, public domain, monthly): statewide confirmation, "serves alcohol",
  and the brewpub guide (Brew Pub and Distillery Pub licenses).
- **Denver Active Business Licenses** (City and County of Denver, daily): Retail Food, Combined and Liquor licenses.
- Every other county publishes inspections one search at a time; those portals are not scraped.

## Hand-checked research
`data/research/` (private): MICHELIN Guide Colorado 2026 (63 restaurants), James Beard honors 2023–26, and regional lists of green chile,
game and steakhouses, ski-town dining and the oldest places, each with a 2025–26 source; closures with sources; leads that couldn't be
verified (`data/research/app/LEADS_*.md`).
