Oldest restaurants in Breckenridge

Founding years at the current address, as each place or a published history documents them, oldest first. The note under each says what the year rests on. Statewide: Colorado's oldest restaurants.

  1. Gold Pan Saloon

    103 N Main St, Breckenridge

    On the menu: Breckie burger, lamb French dip with goat cheese · Since 1879

    Breckenridge's longest-running saloon, in its 1879 building on Main Street, now pouring drinks and serving burgers and lamb French dips.

    A tent saloon (Long's Saloon) operated here from 1861; the permanent building went up in 1879, and the Gold Pan name dates to 1953.

    thegoldpansaloon.com
