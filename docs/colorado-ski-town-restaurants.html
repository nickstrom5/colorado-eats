Eating in the mountains

Colorado's resort towns have more good restaurants per resident than anywhere else in the state, and many keep their own schedule: on-mountain lodges open only in ski season, and lots of town restaurants close for a few weeks of "mud season" in spring and fall. Those closures are not permanent, and the list marks seasonal places when they say so.

By town: Aspen (7), Telluride (6), Vail (6), Durango (5), Breckenridge (4), Crested Butte (4), Avon (3), Steamboat Springs (3), Winter Park (2), Edwards (2).

How the list was built: each place was checked against a 2025 or 2026 source, usually its own site or menu or a dated story in the local paper. Places with no current source were left out. MICHELIN Guide Colorado restaurants in these towns are included. The order is by region and town, never by rating.
