Boulder County restaurant inspections

The latest recorded inspection result for 340 restaurants, cafés and bars in Boulder County, from Boulder County Public Health's records through July 21, 2026: the result, the risk points and the date. We show the results as recorded and never compute a grade of our own.

How Colorado scores an inspection

Colorado's retail food inspections, set by the Colorado Department of Public Health and Environment, end in one of three results: Pass (0–49 risk points), Re-Inspection Required (50–109) or Closure (110+ points). Boulder County also records a Closure when it closes a place for an imminent health hazard, such as a sewage backup, whatever the points. Fewer points is better.

At their latest recorded inspection: 332 places Pass and 8 places Re-Inspection Required. A Re-Inspection Required result means the inspector comes back to check the fixes; it is not a closure.

Each row is one place's latest inspection. One inspection is a snapshot of one day, and the published records can lag behind the county's own files. Boulder County is the only Colorado county that publishes its inspections in bulk, so other counties aren't covered here. Source: Boulder County Public Health on data.colorado.gov (public domain). For the agency's own records, contact Boulder County Public Health.

“Not published” in the Date column marks results Boulder County recorded during its September 2025 move to a new records system, which carry no real inspection date.
