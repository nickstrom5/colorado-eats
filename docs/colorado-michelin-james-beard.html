What's here, and what isn't

These are facts from the honoring organizations, not ratings: a MICHELIN distinction or a James Beard Foundation award or nomination, checked against the official announcements. Restaurants that have closed since their honor are left out. Readers' polls, "best of" lists and star ratings from review sites are never included.

MICHELIN's Colorado guide covered Denver, Boulder and the resort towns from 2023 and expanded statewide in 2026. The Green Star was retired worldwide in June 2026, so none is shown.
