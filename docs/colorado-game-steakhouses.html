Colorado on the plate

Bison and elk ranch across the state, Rocky Mountain trout comes out of its rivers, and steakhouses have fed Denver since the stockyards. The dishes listed with each place are from its current menu when we checked.

How the list was built: each place was checked against a 2025 or 2026 source. Menus change with the season, so check before you go. The order is by region and town, never by rating.
