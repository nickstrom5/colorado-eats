Colorado's oldest restaurants

30 Colorado restaurants and saloons with a documented founding year at their current address, oldest first. 18 opened before 1950. Under each one, a note says what the year rests on.

How a founding year is chosen

A founding year here means the year the place opened at this address in its present form, as the place itself or a published history documents it; for a hotel's bar or dining room that opened with the hotel, the hotel's year. It is never the year a brand started elsewhere, and never a year before a move. Earlier history on the same lot, like a tent saloon, is in the note under the place. When sources disagree we use the later date, and when the year rests on the place's own account, the note under it says so. Each was checked in fall 2026 against a 2025–26 source.

Oldest first

  1. Gold Pan Saloon

    103 N Main St, Breckenridge

    On the menu: Breckie burger, lamb French dip with goat cheese · Since 1879

    Breckenridge's longest-running saloon, in its 1879 building on Main Street, now pouring drinks and serving burgers and lamb French dips.

    A tent saloon (Long's Saloon) operated here from 1861; the permanent building went up in 1879, and the Gold Pan name dates to 1953.

    thegoldpansaloon.com

  2. Lacey Rose Saloon

    1219 Greene St, Silverton

    On the menu: filet mignon, ribeye, chicken-fried steak, half-pound burgers · Since 1882

    Saloon and restaurant inside Silverton's 1882 Grand Imperial Hotel, serving steaks, burgers and chicken-fried steak.

    The Grand Imperial Hotel opened in 1882; its saloon and restaurant now trade as the Lacey Rose Saloon.

    laceyrosesaloon.com

  3. Silver Dollar Saloon

    315 Harrison Ave, Leadville

    Game & steak On the menu: rabbit and rattlesnake sausage, chicken fried yak (seasonal), wings · Since 1883

    Victorian Harrison Avenue saloon from Leadville's silver boom, now with a kitchen serving rabbit-and-rattlesnake sausage and chicken-fried yak.

    The saloon's site says 1879; other histories date it to 1883 as the Board of Trade saloon, renamed Silver Dollar in 1935. We use the later date.

    legendarysilverdollarsaloon.com

  4. J-Bar

    330 E Main St, Aspen

    On the menu: burger, Aspen Crud milkshake · Since 1889

    Wood-paneled bar in Aspen's 1889 Hotel Jerome, a silver-era saloon turned après hangout known for its burger.

    Hotel Jerome and its bar opened in 1889.

  5. Baron's Restaurant at Hotel Colorado

    526 Pine St, Glenwood Springs

    Since 1893

    Restaurant inside Glenwood Springs' grand 1893 Hotel Colorado across from the hot springs pool.

    The hotel has operated since 1893; its main dining room now runs as Baron's Restaurant.

    hotelcolorado.com

  6. Buckhorn Exchange

    1000 Osage St, Denver

    Game & steak On the menu: Rocky Mountain oysters, elk medallions, buffalo prime rib, buffalo tenderloin, smoked buffalo sausage, quail, fried alligator tail, prime steaks · Since 1893

    Lincoln Park steakhouse filled with mounted game and Old West artifacts, serving buffalo, elk, quail and Rocky Mountain oysters alongside prime beef.

    Opened by Henry "Shorty Scout" Zietz in November 1893; its history page says it was issued Colorado Liquor License #1, which is still displayed.

    buckhorn.com

  7. New Sheridan Chop House & Historic Bar

    231 W Colorado Ave, Telluride

    Game & steak On the menu: elk shortloin, dry-aged bison ribeye, Colorado rack of lamb, prime New York strip, prime filet mignon, dry-aged tomahawk · Since 1895

    Telluride's 1895 hotel bar with its original back bar, next to a steakhouse that grills elk, bison and prime beef.

    The hotel's Historic Bar has served since 1895, when the New Sheridan was rebuilt; the Chop House is a later addition in the same building.

  8. Chautauqua Dining Hall

    900 Baseline Rd, Boulder

    Since 1898

    Historic dining hall in Chautauqua Park below the Flatirons, built for the Chautauqua's first season in 1898.

    The dining hall was built for the Colorado Chautauqua's opening season in 1898 and is still at the same address.

    chautauqua.com

  9. The Sink

    1165 13th St, Boulder

    On the menu: burgers, pizza · Since 1923

    University Hill burger-and-pizza spot with hand-painted ceilings, feeding CU students since the 1920s.

    Opened in 1923 as Somers' Sunken Gardens at this address; officially renamed The Sink in 1949. It closed for a time during the COVID-19 pandemic.

    thesink.com

  10. Charlie Brown's Bar & Grill

    980 N Grant St, Denver

    On the menu: green chile, prime rib (Thursdays), Greek specialties, burgers · Since 1933

    Capitol Hill piano bar in the Colburn Hotel with nightly live piano, a long diner-style menu with green chile, and Thursday prime rib.

    Opened December 6, 1933, on the ground floor of the Colburn Hotel; the bar's site cites 93 years in business.

    charliebrownsbarandgrill.com

  11. Silver Grill Cafe

    218 Walnut St, Fort Collins

    Green chile On the menu: pork green chili, breakfast burrito, tamale & eggs, loaded hash browns, cinnamon rolls · Since 1933

    Old Town breakfast-and-lunch cafe known for its giant cinnamon rolls, with Colorado pork green chili on burritos, tamales and hash browns.

    Opened at 218 Walnut St on October 28, 1933; it later expanded into neighboring storefronts but never moved.

    silvergrill.com

  12. The Cruise Room

    1600 17th St, Denver

    On the menu: martinis, small plates · Since 1933

    Art Deco bar inside the Oxford Hotel, modeled on a lounge from the Queen Mary ocean liner, pouring martinis with a small-plates menu.

    Opened inside the Oxford Hotel in 1933, the day after Prohibition was repealed, per the hotel.

    theoxfordhotel.com

  13. Ship Tavern

    321 17th St, Denver

    On the menu: New York strip, petite filet, flatiron steak, oysters · Since 1934

    Nautical-themed tavern inside the Brown Palace, decorated with model ships and brass, serving steaks and seafood.

    Opened in the Brown Palace Hotel in 1934, after Prohibition ended.

    brownpalace.com

  14. The Navajo Hogan

    2817 N Nevada Ave, Colorado Springs

    Since 1935

    Log roadhouse on North Nevada, listed on the National Register of Historic Places, restored and reopened in 2026 along with its neon sign.

    Built in 1935 as a roadside bar and restaurant at this address. It was closed from 2021 to 2026 after the pandemic and two fires, and reopened in 2026 under new owners.

    thenavajohogan.com

  15. The Rock Inn Mountain Tavern

    1675 CO-66, Estes Park

    BrewpubGame & steak On the menu: smoked elk sausage, Colorado bison meatballs, bison marinara, pan-seared trout · Since 1937

    Mountain tavern on Highway 66 outside Estes Park with a menu of smoked elk sausage, bison dishes and pan-seared trout.

    The tavern's site gives 1937 as its founding year at this location.

    rockinnestes.com

  16. Cherry Cricket

    2641 E 2nd Ave, Denver

    Green chileGame & steak On the menu: pork green chili, veggie green chili, 303 green chile relleno burger, Colorado bison burger, grande burrito with pork green chili · Since 1945

    Cherry Creek burger bar serving pork green chili by the bowl and as a burger topping, plus a Colorado bison burger.

    Opened at this address in 1945 as Mary Zimmerman's bar and renamed Cherry Cricket in 1950; it was rebuilt in place after a 2016 kitchen fire and reopened in 2017.

    cherrycricket.com

  17. Don's Club Tavern

    723 E 6th Ave, Denver

    Since 1947

    Old-school neighborhood tavern near Governor's Park that stays open until 2 a.m. daily.

    Opened in 1947; the bar says it has changed little since then.

    donsclubtavern.com

  18. Gaetano's Italian

    3760 Tejon St, Denver

    On the menu: meatballs, lobster ravioli, lasagna, chicken parmesan, gnocchi · Since 1947

    Red-sauce Italian restaurant in a brick building in Highland with long ties to the Smaldone family.

    Opened on Tejon Street in 1947 by the Smaldone family; current owner since 2013.

    gaetanositalian.com

  19. Charco Broiler

    1716 E Mulberry St, Fort Collins

    Game & steak On the menu: hand-cut steaks, ribeye, New York strip, chicken fried steak, burgers · Since 1957

    McGarvey family steakhouse on East Mulberry serving hand-cut steaks, burgers and weekend breakfast for three generations.

    Opened in fall 1957 as a coffee shop and burger joint and later became a steakhouse; a 2017 list of Fort Collins' oldest restaurants says it is still at its original location.

  20. Davies' Chuck Wagon Diner

    9495 W Colfax Ave, Lakewood

    Green chile On the menu: green chile smothered breakfast burrito, green chili eggs Benedict, country fried steak, Chuckwagon breakfast · Since 1957

    Stainless-steel 1957 diner under a giant neon cowboy, serving breakfast burritos smothered in green chile and country fried steak.

    A prefabricated diner built in New Jersey, shipped by rail and opened on this West Colfax site in 1957 by Lyman Davies; listed on the National Register.

    davies-chuckwagon.com

  21. Diamond Belle Saloon

    699 Main Ave, Durango

    On the menu: green chile burger, filet mignon, burgers · Since 1957

    Victorian saloon inside Durango's 1887 Strater Hotel, revived in 1957, with live music nightly and a burger-and-entree menu.

    The Strater Hotel dates to 1887; the Diamond Belle Saloon inside it was revived as a saloon in 1957.

    strater.com

  22. Bastien's Restaurant

    3503 E Colfax Ave, Denver

    Game & steak On the menu: sugar steak, filet mignon, New York strip, porterhouse, bone-in ribeye, dry-aged ribeye · Since 1958

    Family-run mid-century steakhouse on East Colfax under a folded-plate roof, known for its sugar-rubbed steak.

    The Bastien family ran the Moon Drive Inn on this corner from 1937 (hence the restaurant's 'since 1937' line); they tore it down and opened Bastien's in the current Googie-style building in 1958, which is on the National Register.

    bastiensrestaurant.com

  23. Columbine Steak House & Lounge

    300 Federal Blvd, Denver

    Game & steak On the menu: steaks with baked potato and Texas toast · Since 1961

    Cash-only, order-at-the-counter steakhouse on Federal Boulevard with budget steak dinners.

    Open on Federal Boulevard since 1961.

    columbinesteakhouseandlounge.com

  24. Pepi's Restaurant & Bar

    231 Gore Creek Dr, Vail

    Game & steak On the menu: wild game dinners (Antlers Room), Austrian dishes · Since 1964 · Season: Antlers Room game dinners run in the winter season

    Austrian-style hotel restaurant in Vail Village from the town's first years, with a wood-paneled Antlers Room known for wild game dinners in winter.

    Gasthof Gramshammer was established by the Gramshammer family in 1964, in Vail's early years.

    pepis.com

  25. The Original Chubby's

    1231 W 38th Ave, Denver

    Green chile On the menu: Mexican hamburger, green chile · Since 1967

    Northside walk-up Den-Mex stand whose green chile has smothered its Mexican hamburger since the 1960s.

    Open on West 38th Avenue since 1967.

  26. Outlaw Restaurant

    610 Main St, Ouray

    Game & steak On the menu: prime rib, ribeye, New York strip, filet mignon, Colorado lamb chops, trout San Juan · Since 1968

    Ouray Main Street steakhouse since the late 1960s, serving prime rib, Colorado lamb chops and Rocky Mountain trout.

    Site says its front sign went up in 1968.

    outlawrestaurant.com

  27. My Brother's Bar

    2376 15th St, Denver

    Game & steak On the menu: JCB (jalapeño cream cheese burger), Ralphie bison burger, Steerburger · Since 1970

    Unsigned corner tavern at 15th and Platte with classical music on the stereo, known for its JCB burger and a bison burger.

    Renamed My Brother's Bar by the Karagas brothers in 1970. The bar says a saloon has served at this corner since 1873 under other names, last as Whitey's; that claim spans Prohibition and isn't independently verified, so the year shown is 1970.

    mybrothersbar.com

  28. Ore House at the Pine Grove

    1465 Pine Grove Rd, Steamboat Springs

    Game & steak On the menu: peppered elk loin, bison ribeye, Rocky Mountain ruby red trout, steaks, salad bar, cinnamon rolls · Since 1971

    Steamboat steak and game house in an old ranch barn since 1971, with elk loin, bison ribeye and trout alongside its salad bar.

    Opened in 1971 in a converted barn more than a century old.

    orehouse.com

  29. Gold Dust Saloon

    217 S Union Ave, Pueblo

    Green chile On the menu: slopper, mac 'n' cheese slopper, chile relleno slopper, green chile burgers · Since 1984

    Union Avenue saloon and grill with several slopper variations, green chile burgers and craft beer.

    The saloon's own site says it has been open since 1984.

    golddustsaloon.net

  30. El Taco de Mexico

    714 Santa Fe Dr, Denver

    America's ClassicGreen chile On the menu: green chile bowl, smothered burritos · Since 1985

    Santa Fe Drive counter spot, a 2020 James Beard America's Classic, making green chile daily for its smothered burritos.

    Opened in 1985 by Maria Luisa Zanabria.
